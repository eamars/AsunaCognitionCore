# Not gaps of the stand-in: one defect class its speed shows. This machine's clock moves in steps of 1 ms, and the
# stand-in writes faster than that, so two records written one after the other can carry the same time; code that
# decides by "the latest" or "the oldest" time then decides by whatever breaks the tie (often a hash _id). On the

# within one millisecond (a tight loop over a LAN round trip): rare, possible. The fix is the class's, not each
