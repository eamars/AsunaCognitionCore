# ADR-123: The person layer as items

Status: **Proposed** 2026-11-09 (laid down; not designed, not built). Moved out of ADR-117 (§11); this is ADR-062's
phase 3. The owner, 2026-11-05: phase 3 is to be treated carefully and discussed with him before any build.

## 1. The direction (decided in ADR-062's review, D5a)

One document per person, with its sections as items and a 4,000-character carried budget per person. The anchored
别人说的 sections and her promises lapse last, and in groups the anchored sections ride beside the short.

## 2. What its design must solve (ADR-117 §11, given in full there)

1. The home-only fence, per (person, visibility).
2. Reading by name, under `understanding.resolve`'s rule.
3. No stay lapse for the person store.
4. Erasure of the tier, move, index and pair rows.
5. Its overflow: the key, where the choice line appears, and the tie-break.
6. Groups: only the woken person.
7. A bounded transition from the old `relationship:…|person` heads.
8. Moved and came-back lines appear only with that person.
9. The write side, through `understand_person`.
10. Her answers: her promise word, which records she moves and when, and whether the here layer follows.

## 3. Closes when

A design that answers all ten points is discussed with the owner and read by her, then built. Each fence is shown by
a staged case: a home-only section in a public turn with that person, a read by name of someone absent, and an
erasure.

- Whether the owner's own person record (3,563 of 4,000 characters on 2026-11-05) needs anything at the transition.
- Its order against ADR-119 and ADR-120.
